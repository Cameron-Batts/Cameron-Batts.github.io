Megacorp Acquisition Scenario Analysis

Author: Cameron Batts

Tools: SAS Visual Analytics / SAS Viya

Aggregate validation record.

Problem

Explore operational reliability, facility geography and product-line profit in a fictional acquisition scenario.

Approach

Review Exploration, Geography and Profit Analysis pages, including the PDF appendix that records object-specific year filters. Separate the headline KPI from the year-filtered product and trend objects.

Findings

The export displays a rounded $51M headline profit KPI, whose filter scope is not documented. The product-line chart is explicitly filtered to 2018: Game approximately $1.8M, Promotional $878K, Stuffed Animal -$219K and Action Figure -$1.5M. The year trend shows approximately $4.9M in 2015, $4M in 2016, -$189K in 2017 and $972K in 2018. The rounded product-line values sum to about $959K, broadly consistent with the $972K year point, not the $51M KPI.

Limitations

The $51M KPI must not be labeled 2018 profit. Its scope cannot be reconciled without the source and report definition. The visible facility table is a partial view, not proof of exactly 12 facilities. Correlation cells do not establish causal drivers, and this dashboard is not a valuation, due diligence conclusion or real acquisition outcome.

Data and provenance

MEGACORP2020 is identified in the original documentation as a SAS Viya for Learners dataset for a fictional company. The documented 2,229,087 rows and 32 columns are not independently verified because the dataset is absent.

Verification

PDF pages and repository assets reviewed; source data and native SAS interactions are unavailable.
